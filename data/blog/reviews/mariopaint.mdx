### Unexpected Game Add
This is just a fun, quick review of Mario Paint, which was recently added to the
Nintendo Online subscription. I didn’t expect to see this one ported over, but it 
definitely brings back memories. It has all the cheesy mini-games I remember—swatting
 bugs and putting on a musical.

You can also create artwork, though I was never very good at that part.

I used the built-in "mouse" feature on the Switch controller. It works okay, 
but it doesn’t feel as good as using a real mouse. That said, I 3D printed a 
“controller upgrade” that makes the controller feel a bit more like a regular 
mouse—and that helped.

Here is the [3D model](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1455279-switch-2-joycon-2-mouse-adapter-ergonomic-design?from=search#profileId-1516600) I used.
